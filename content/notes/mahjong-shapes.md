## 5-tile completed shapes

### 3-1-1

Example: 22234p = 22p 234p

### 2-1-1-1

Example: 22345p = 22p 345p

## 6-tile completed shapes (2 toitsu)

### 3-3

Example: 222333p = 222p 333p

### 2-2-2

Example: 223344p = 234p 234p

### 1-2-2-1

Example: 233445p = 234p 345p

## 8-tile completed shapes

### 2-3-3

Example: 22333444p = 22p 333p 444p

### 2-2-2-2

Example: 22334455p = 22p 345p 345p

### 2-1-2-2-1

Example: 22344556p = 22p 345p 456p

## 9-tile completed shapes (3 toitsu)

### 1-3-3-2 (並び暗刻に2個・1個)

Example: 23344455p = 234p 345p 345p

### 1-2-3-2-1 (Pyramid)

Example: 233444556p = 234p 345p 456p

# Multiple suji wait

Most of the time, your wait is confined to just one suji.
But there are times when your wait spans multiple suji, but there are certain criteria to enable this.

1. Your shape contains a head
	1. e.g. There are either 4, 7, or 10 tiles
2. There is a triplet in your shape

### Example: 3456667p

34567p 66p is waiting for 258p
But 345p 666p 7p is waiting for 7p
